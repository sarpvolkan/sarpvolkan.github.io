I am a **space weather researcher** with a background in systems engineering. I combine **signal processing, complexity science, and physics-based modeling** to understand the ionosphere and its interactions with the Sun and upper atmosphere.

My work turns large geophysical datasets into insight through multiscale analysis, nonlinear forecasting, and computational methods adapted across disciplines. My goal is to develop physically meaningful, explainable methods that support space weather forecasting and monitoring.

## Selected Work

### Nonlinear Solar-Cycle Forecasting

Developed a data-driven forecasting approach using time-delay embedding and simplex projection to predict the amplitude and timing of Solar Cycle 25.

[View publication →](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty2470)

### Automated Solar Feature Tracking

Adapted a Lagrangian particle-tracking algorithm from biomedical imaging to detect and track fine-scale solar features throughout their lifetimes in high-resolution Goode Solar Telescope observations.

[View publication →](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11207-020-01618-y)
